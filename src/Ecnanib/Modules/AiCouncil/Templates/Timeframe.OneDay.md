# Timeframe: 1D

- **Higher-timeframe context:** the weekly trend, from the 1W indicators and candles, and the 200-day average, which is `ema200` in the 1D indicators.
- **Typical holding period:** weeks to months.
- Use trend-following and breakout logic: trade with the weekly trend and the 200-day average, and enter on breakouts or on pullbacks within the trend.
- Expect few signals and a low win rate, offset by large winners. Holding is the usual answer, and it isn't a missed opportunity.
- Use wide stops, around 3 × ATR(14) of the daily candles, and trailing exits: let winners run, and move the stop only to lock in gains.
- On Futures, funding is a major cost over a long hold, and long exposure may be cheaper on Spot.
