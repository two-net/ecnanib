# Timeframe: 15m

- **Higher-timeframe context:** the 1H trend, from the 1H indicators and candles.
- **Typical holding period:** minutes to hours.
- Trade only in the direction of the 1H trend. When the 1H trend is a range, wait for a clean setup at its edges or hold.
- Fees and spread are large relative to 15m moves, so skip setups with a low reward-to-risk, and prefer limit entries to market entries.
- Avoid new entries around major scheduled economic releases, such as US CPI, FOMC decisions and non-farm payrolls, when price can jump through stops.
