# Timeframe: 4H

- **Higher-timeframe context:** the daily trend, such as price against the 200-day average, which is `ema200` in the 1D indicators.
- **Typical holding period:** days to weeks.
- Look for swing setups: pullbacks in a trend, and breakouts from a range that the daily trend supports.
- Use time-based exits: a trade that stalls for several candles without moving toward its target has lost its reason, so consider closing it or tightening its stop.
- Treat correlated assets as one bet: most crypto assets move with Bitcoin, so a market-wide move matters more than this pair's own story.
- On Futures, account for the funding paid over a multi-day hold, which can be a real share of the expected gain.
