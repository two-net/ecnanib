# Timeframe: 1H

- **Higher-timeframe context:** the daily trend, from the 1D indicators and candles.
- **Typical holding period:** hours to days.
- Trade in the direction of the daily trend.
- Account for overnight and weekend exposure: a position can be held through thin liquidity and gaps, so the stop must be one you'd accept being hit while nobody is watching.
- Prefer fewer, higher-quality setups. Missing a trade costs nothing.
